Quick answer

The closest metro station to Liwan Lake Park (荔湾湖公园) is Zhongshanba (中山八路), served by Line 5 and the Line 11 loop — a 5–10 minute walk from the park's north and west gates. From Guangzhou Railway Station it is three stops on Line 5 (about 10 minutes); from Baiyun Airport, allow 60–90 minutes with two transfers. The Guangzhou Metro averaged 9.31 million trips per day in 2025, so timing your ride matters.

Part 1: Journey times to Liwan Lake Park (Zhongshanba Station)

From Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport (CAN)

  • Route: Line 3 (North Extension) from the airport → transfer at Jiahewanggang to Line 2 → transfer at Guangzhou Railway Station to Line 5 → Zhongshanba.
  • Total time: 60–90 minutes including transfer walks.
  • Cost: under ¥10; buy a token or use the metro QR code in WeChat/Alipay — confirm the exact fare in the app on your travel day.

From Guangzhou Railway Station (main national rail hub)

  • Route: Line 5 westbound, three stops — Xicun → Xichang → Zhongshanba.
  • Total time: about 10 minutes of riding, then a 5–10 minute walk to the park's north or west gate.
  • Cost: a couple of yuan — the cheapest leg of the trip.

Tip: if you arrive in Guangzhou by train at the main station, this is the fastest and simplest way to reach the old town — no transfers, one line.

Part 2: Guangzhou Metro, five years in numbers (2021–2025)

These figures come from Guangzhou Metro Group's published operations summaries and the China Association of Metros annual statistics — see the sources section below. Where official figures were not available, we say so rather than estimate.

YearNetwork context
2021Post-pandemic recovery baseline: ridership climbed back steadily through the year as domestic travel resumed. Official daily-average figures for this year are not consistently published; we do not quote an estimate.
2022Operations normalized with continued expansion of the network into the western districts, improving access to the old town. Year-level official averages are likewise not consistently published.
2023Full reopening of international travel: holiday peaks regularly pushed daily network traffic past the 10 million mark, with heritage areas such as Liwan and Yongqingfang among the busiest destinations.
2024Line 11 — Guangzhou's first circular loop line — opened in December 2024, directly linking the eastern business districts to the old town and stopping at Zhongshanba. The network averaged 8.879 million trips per day for the year.
2025Record maturity: 3.399 billion annual trips on the metro network, a daily average of 9.312 million (+4.9% year on year), and 89 days above 10 million. On December 31, 2025 the network set a single-day record of 14.093 million trips — also a national metro record. Network mileage reached about 780 km.

The busiest lines — and what they mean for your visit

  • Line 3 is the system's heaviest line (well over a million trips on an average day), followed by Line 2 (~1.16M) and Line 5 (~1.01M) — the very lines you will ride from the airport and railway station. Avoid 7:30–9:00 and 17:30–19:30 with luggage.
  • Line 11, in its first full year (2025), averaged roughly 550,000 trips per day and removed entire transfers from cross-town journeys — for visitors coming from Tianhe, reaching the old town no longer requires threading the Line 3 crush.
  • Holiday peaks (Golden Week, New Year's Eve) push the whole network past 10–14 million trips a day; the stations around Liwan fill early on those dates.

Practical tips for riding to the park

  • Start your park visit early: ride before 8:30 on a weekday and you will share the train with commuters but exit into the park's best hour — tai chi, opera warm-ups and mist on the lake.
  • Use the Guangzhou Metro QR code inside WeChat or Alipay; station machines also sell tokens with an English interface.
  • Pin 中山八路站 or the park's North Gate (北门) in Amap for door-to-gate directions.
  • On December 31 and during Golden Week, expect station crowd control (queues outside the gates) — build in 15 extra minutes.

FAQ

Which metro station is closest to Liwan Lake Park?

Zhongshanba (中山八路, Lines 5 and 11) — about a 5–10 minute walk from the north and west gates.

How do I get from Baiyun Airport to the park by metro?

Line 3 (North Extension) → Line 2 at Jiahewanggang → Line 5 at Guangzhou Railway Station → Zhongshanba. Allow 60–90 minutes including transfers.

How far is the park from Guangzhou Railway Station?

Three stops on Line 5 (Xicun, Xichang, Zhongshanba) — about 10 minutes of riding plus a 5–10 minute walk.

Is the metro crowded?

Yes — the network averaged 9.31 million trips per day in 2025. Avoid rush hours (7:30–9:00, 17:30–19:30) with luggage; midday and early morning are far calmer.

Does Line 11 help visitors?

Very much — the loop line opened in December 2024 and connects the eastern districts directly to Zhongshanba, cutting out transfers through the busiest interchange stations.

What should I do after arriving at Zhongshanba?

Exit toward the park's north gate, walk the lake loop, and continue into Xiguan — see the Xiguan walking tour and the Xiguan food guide.
