How to Get to Liwan Lake Park in Guangzhou: Metro, Bus, Taxi, Walking Routes

Last updated: September 2026

Quick answer

The easiest way to reach Liwan Lake Park (荔湾湖公园) is the Guangzhou Metro to Ruyifang (如意坊, Line 6) or Changshou Lu (长寿路, Line 1), then a 10–20 minute walk or short taxi ride. From Shangxiajiu Pedestrian Street you can simply walk. From the airport or South Station, allow 60–90 minutes.

Buy tokens from machines (English available) or use the Guangzhou Metro QR code in WeChat/Alipay. Search 荔湾湖公园 in Amap for door-to-gate walking directions.

By bus

Multiple Liwan District bus routes stop along Longjin West Road (龙津西路) and nearby streets within a few minutes' walk of park entrances. Routes change periodically — check Amap or Baidu Maps for live routes and stops on your travel day.

By taxi / Didi

Show the driver: 荔湾湖公园（荔湾区龙津西路）. From Zhujiang New Town expect 40–70 minutes; from Shamian about 10–15 minutes. Didi (滴滴) works with an English interface and international cards.

Walking routes

From Shangxiajiu Pedestrian Street (10–20 min)

Leave the arcade at its north end, follow signs toward Longjin West Road through the old-town lanes, and you reach the park's eastern entrances. This is the classic pairing: shops and snacks first, lake calm after.

From Yongqingfang (10–20 min)

Exit the heritage lanes toward Enning Road and continue west — the walk itself, past Qilou shophouses, is part of the experience.

Practical tips

  • Avoid metro rush hours (7:30–9:00, 17:30–19:30) with luggage.
  • Amap (高德地图) is the most reliable navigation app in Guangzhou; download offline maps.
  • Rain changes everything: allow extra time and prefer metro over taxi during downpours.
  • Golden Week (early October): expect queues at nearby metro stations.
