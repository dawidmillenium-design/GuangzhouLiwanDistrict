Liwan Lake Park, Guangzhou — the old-town lake park where Cantonese culture is still alive

A complete, field-researched visitor guide to Liwanhu Park: how to get there, when to go, where to photograph pavilions and lotus ponds, and how to combine it with Shangxiajiu and Yongqingfang.

Also available in Русский · 简体中文

Quick answer: Liwan Lake Park (Liwanhu Park, 荔湾湖公园) is a public lake park in Guangzhou's historic Liwan District. Built around a chain of lakes in the old Xiguan quarter, it is known for traditional pavilions, arched bridges, morning Cantonese opera singing, and an unhurried local atmosphere — an easy half-day combined with Shangxiajiu Pedestrian Street and Yongqingfang.
