Quick answer

Boat rentals at Liwan Lake Park start from around 70 RMB per person for a pedal boat and 100 RMB per person for an electric boat, each lasting about one hour, while traditional sampan rides on Litchi Bay Creek cost roughly 50 RMB per person for 30 minutes. Rentals are available at the park's docks; arrive early on weekends, bring sun protection, and confirm current prices before boarding.

There is no better way to experience Liwan Lake Park than from the water. The park's four interconnected lakes, framed by weeping willows and traditional Lingnan architecture, offer a perspective that cannot be fully appreciated from the shore. Whether you choose a leisurely pedal boat, a convenient electric boat, or a traditional sampan along Litchi Bay Creek, boating is an essential part of the Liwan Lake experience.

This guide covers everything you need to know about boat rentals at Liwan Lake Park, including current prices, boat types, rental procedures, and practical tips for making the most of your time on the water.

Types of Boats Available

Liwan Lake Park offers several types of boats to suit different preferences and group sizes.

Pedal Boats (脚踏船)

Pedal boats are the classic choice for families and couples who want to enjoy a leisurely pace on the water. These four-seater boats are simple to operate and provide a gentle workout while you take in the scenery. The pedal boat experience is ideal for first-time visitors who want to explore the lakes at their own speed, stopping to photograph lotus blossoms or drifting beneath arched bridges.

Electric Boats (电动船)

Electric boats offer a more relaxed alternative to pedal boats. These boats are motorized, requiring no physical effort to navigate. They are particularly popular among older visitors and those who want to cover more ground in less time. An electric boat allows you to glide effortlessly across the lakes, enjoying the breeze and the views without breaking a sweat.

Traditional Sampans (小舢板)

For a truly atmospheric experience, consider a traditional sampan ride along Litchi Bay Creek. These small wooden boats, operated by local boatmen, offer a nostalgic journey through the waterways that once served as the main transportation routes of Xiguan. The boatmen often share stories and legends about the area, adding a cultural dimension to the ride.

The Boating Experience

When you rent a boat at Liwan Lake Park, you are not just renting a vehicle; you are renting an experience. The lakes are interconnected, allowing you to navigate from one to another and discover hidden corners of the park that are inaccessible on foot.

As you paddle or glide along, you will pass beneath graceful stone bridges, glide past traditional pavilions reflected in the water, and drift through areas where lotus flowers bloom in profusion during the summer months. The best time to go boating is early morning, when the light is soft and the park is relatively quiet, or late afternoon, when the setting sun casts a golden glow over the water.

Practical Tips for Boating

  • Arrive Early: Boat rentals can get busy, especially on weekends and holidays. Arriving early in the morning gives you the best selection of boats and a quieter experience on the water.
  • Bring Sun Protection: The lakes offer little shade while you are on the water. Hats, sunscreen, and sunglasses are essential, particularly during the summer months.
  • Check the Weather: Boating is not advisable during rainstorms or strong winds. If the weather looks uncertain, check with the rental staff before committing.
  • Keep Valuables Secure: Water splashes are possible, especially in pedal boats. Keep phones and cameras in waterproof bags or secure pockets.
  • Respect the Environment: Do not litter from the boats, and avoid disturbing the lotus flowers and other aquatic plants.

Beyond Liwan Lake: The Litchi Bay Creek Experience

For a different boating experience, consider the sampan rides along Litchi Bay Creek. This waterway, which connects to Liwan Lake, offers a more intimate journey through the historic Xiguan waterways. The creek passes beneath ancient bridges and alongside traditional Xiguan mansions, providing a window into the daily life and architectural heritage of old Guangzhou.

The sampan ride is a particularly good option for photographers, as the slower pace and closer proximity to the banks allow for more detailed shots of the traditional architecture and the bustling life along the creek.

Conclusion

Boat rental at Liwan Lake Park is an affordable and memorable way to experience one of Guangzhou's most beautiful public spaces. Whether you choose a pedal boat for a gentle workout, an electric boat for effortless cruising, or a sampan for a cultural journey, the experience of seeing Liwan Lake from the water will be one of the highlights of your visit to Guangzhou.
