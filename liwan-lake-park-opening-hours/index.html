Liwan Lake Park Opening Hours, Ticket Info, and Visitor Rules

Last updated: September 2026

Quick answer

Liwan Lake Park (荔湾湖公园) is a public city park that has generally been free to enter, open from early morning into the evening. There is no gate ticket for the grounds; separate paid activities (pedal boats, some exhibitions) post their own prices on-site. Policies can change — verify locally before visiting.

Important: Guangzhou parks occasionally adjust gate times seasonally or for maintenance and events. Treat the pattern above as guidance, and check the notice boards at park entrances or recent visitor reports for exact times on your date.

Tickets and costs

  • Park entry: historically free; no advance booking needed.
  • Pedal boats: rented at lake piers, typically by the half hour; pay by WeChat Pay/Alipay or cash. Prices are posted at the pier.
  • Exhibitions/events: occasional paid exhibitions inside park buildings post their own fees.

Visitor rules and etiquette

  • No swimming or fishing in the lake.
  • Keep noise respectful near opera and music circles; listen before photographing.
  • Drones are generally restricted over crowded urban parks in Guangzhou — check current local drone rules before flying.
  • Pets: rules vary; expect leashes required at minimum.
  • Take litter with you; bins are at plazas and entrances.

FAQ

Is Liwan Lake Park free?

Yes, the grounds have generally been free. Paid extras (boats, some exhibitions) are charged separately. Verify on-site.

Do I need to book in advance?

No booking is needed for general entry to this public park.

Can I enter before sunrise?

Locals do — the grounds function as a neighborhood park from very early morning. Lighting is minimal at that hour.

Is the park open at night?

Main paths are lit in the evening and the park stays open after dark, but services (boats, restrooms) may close earlier.

What if it rains?

The park stays open in normal rain and is atmospheric then. During typhoon warnings, follow official closure notices.
