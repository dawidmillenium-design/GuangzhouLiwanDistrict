Liwan Lake Park vs Yuexiu Park: Which Guangzhou Park Should You Visit?

Last updated: September 2026

Quick answer

Choose Yuexiu Park if you want Guangzhou's landmark monuments (Five Rams Statue, Zhenhai Tower) and a big hilly park. Choose Liwan Lake Park if you want everyday Cantonese culture — opera singing, lake pavilions, old-town atmosphere — plus the city's best snack streets a short walk away. With a full day, do Liwanhu in the morning and Yuexiu in the afternoon.

Choose Liwan Lake Park if…

  • You care more about living culture than monuments.
  • You photograph people, reflections and details.
  • You want to eat extremely well before or after.
  • You travel with seniors or small kids (flat paths).
  • You have half a day, not a full one.

Choose Yuexiu Park if…

  • It's your first Guangzhou trip and you want the city's iconic statue and tower.
  • You enjoy long hilly walks and forested space.
  • You want direct metro access with minimal navigation.

One-day plan covering both

  1. 7:30 — Liwan Lake Park: morning opera circles and lake reflections.
  2. 9:30 — Xiguan dim sum breakfast.
  3. 11:00 — Shangxiajiu arcades and Yongqingfang lanes.
  4. 14:00 — metro/taxi to Yuexiu Park for the Five Rams Statue and Zhenhai Tower.
  5. 17:00 — back to Liwan for golden-hour pavilion shots and dinner in Xiguan.
